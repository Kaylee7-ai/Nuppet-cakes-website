    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Which flavours are available?

    Pick any flavour in the builder and we will confirm what is available for your date.

    How far ahead should I order?

    Add your date in the builder and we will confirm the notice period for your design.

    Do you require a deposit?

    Deposit and payment details are confirmed with you when your quote is agreed.

    Can I request delivery?

    Yes, ask when you send your enquiry and we will confirm delivery options and timing.

    Can you make allergy-aware cakes?

    Tell us about any allergies in the notes box so we can advise you before you order.

    What if I need to change my order?

    Message us as early as you can and we will confirm what is possible.
